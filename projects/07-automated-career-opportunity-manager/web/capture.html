Keep a job before you lose the tab.

Drag this button to your browser’s bookmarks bar. On a job page, select the description you want to keep, then click the bookmark.

Save to CareerFlow

CareerFlow opens a review form with the page title, link and selected text. Add the company and choose Saved opportunity or I applied. Nothing is stored until you save.

The bookmark sends these fields only to your local CareerFlow app. Keep Docker running. Some sites block bookmark scripts; you can always use Quick capture in Today and paste the link.
